Q & A

Got questions?

If you've got questions, friends and family, have a look here first!

We love your little ones, however this is an adults-only evening. We hope it gives you a chance to let your hair down and celebrate with us properly — we really appreciate your understanding.

There are plentiful spaces in the council carparks adjacent to Zest on Spit Road. Parking is free from 6:00 pm, with over 300 spaces available. That said, a taxi or rideshare home is always a good idea after a big night!

A chuppah is a traditional Jewish wedding canopy under which the couple is married. It symbolises the home and life the couple will build together. Our ceremony will incorporate this beautiful tradition as part of our celebration.

We're having an unplugged ceremony — we kindly ask that phones and cameras are put away during the chuppah so we can all be fully present together. Our photographer will capture every moment beautifully. Photos are absolutely welcome at the reception!

Please reach out directly to Jess and Daniel via email jessicadaniel181026@gmail.com and we'll be happy to help. We can't wait to celebrate with you!

Travel & Stay

Coming from out of town?

Here's where we'd suggest staying, depending on the vibe you're after.

Sydney CBD

Ideal for central location, easy transport connections, and the widest variety of hotels.

Inner East

Surry Hills, Darlinghurst, Paddington and surrounds — perfect if you love a trendy, neighbourhood feel filled with boutique stays, cosy wine bars, and incredible dining.

Eastern Suburbs

Bondi, Double Bay, Coogee, Bronte and surrounds — highly recommended if you want to be near the coastal cliff walks and beach views. October weather here is spectacular!

Northern Beaches

Manly is a fantastic seaside option, offering a relaxed coastal vibe with a direct 20-minute ferry ride across the harbour to the city.
